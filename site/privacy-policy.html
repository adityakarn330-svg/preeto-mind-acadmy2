1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PreetoMind Academy (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and handles personal information shared with us through this website, particularly the Admission Enquiry form, and through our direct communication channels.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an admission enquiry, we collect the information you provide:

  • Student’s full name
  • Parent / guardian name
  • Student’s class or course
  • School or college name
  • Subjects of interest
  • Parent’s mobile number
  • Email address
  • Preferred class timing
  • Current city
  • Any additional message or query you choose to include

When you apply to join our team, we collect your name, email address, mobile number, city, education details, the team and role you are applying for, your skills, availability, any link you share and your message.

We may also receive information you share when you contact us by WhatsApp or email.

3. How Forms Are Delivered

Our website forms are delivered to the academy’s email address through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-forwarding service. The details you enter are transmitted to FormSubmit solely so that they can be emailed to us; we do not use the service for any other purpose. Please review FormSubmit’s own privacy policy for details of its processing.

4. Why We Collect This Information

We collect this information to understand the student’s academic requirements and to respond to your enquiry. It helps us suggest a suitable course, arrange a demo class, and discuss course details and fees with you directly.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your admission enquiry and academic questions
  • To contact you about course details, demo classes and the admission process
  • For enrolled students: to share class routines, academic planners, Google Classroom access, study materials, progress reports and academic updates
  • To communicate with parents about student progress

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for unrelated marketing.

6. How Information Is Handled and Protected

Enquiry information is received by the academy through [name the actual service, e.g. Google Forms / Google Sheets] and email, and it is accessed only by the founder or people authorised by the academy for academic and administrative purposes. We take reasonable care to keep this information private. However, no online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Google Classroom and Google Meet are provided by Google. Your use of those services is also subject to Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

7. Information Displayed Publicly

We do not display students’ or parents’ personal information publicly without appropriate permission. Testimonials, names, photographs or student work are published only with the consent of the student and/or parent, as appropriate.

8. How Long We Keep Information

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to respond to the enquiry and, for enrolled students, for academic and administrative purposes. [Founder to state actual retention period, if any]

9. Corrections and Deletion

Parents or students may ask us to correct their personal information or, where applicable, to delete it. To make a request, contact us at preetomindacadmy@gmail.com or on WhatsApp. We will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children’s Information

Many of our students are minors. Enquiries for students under 18 should be submitted by, or with the knowledge of, a parent or guardian.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact PreetoMind Academy at preetomindacadmy@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +91 79790 73971.
