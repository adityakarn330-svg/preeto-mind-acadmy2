Join Our Team

PreetoMind Academy invites motivated students to join our Tech Team and Teaching Team. Learn by doing, contribute to real education work, and grow with a founder-led academy.

Student Opportunities

Work With Us While You Study

We are building a small, committed team of students who care about education. Whether you are good with technology and design or you love teaching and your subject, there is a place for you to contribute and learn.

Roles are designed around student schedules, with clear tasks and direct mentoring from the founder. You will gain practical experience, build a portfolio and make a real difference to young learners.

Tech Team

Technology, Design & Digital Roles

For students who enjoy building, designing and organising things online.

Website & Google Sites Assistant

Help maintain and update the academy website, add new pages and resources, and keep links, forms and content accurate.

What we look for

  • Basic understanding of websites or Google Sites
  • Attention to detail
  • Comfortable with Google Workspace (Docs, Drive, Forms)

Design & Visual Content Creator

Create clean, on-brand graphics for study materials, social media and the website: posters, worksheet layouts, diagrams and simple illustrations.

What we look for

  • Canva, Figma or similar design tools
  • Good sense of layout and typography
  • Interest in educational and Biology illustrations

Social Media & Digital Outreach

Plan and schedule posts, write short educational captions, and help the academy reach students and parents online.

What we look for

  • Clear written English
  • Familiarity with Instagram, YouTube or LinkedIn
  • Consistency and basic analytics awareness

Tech & Classroom Support

Support the smooth running of live classes and Google Classroom: scheduling, resource uploads, attendance sheets and simple automation with Google Sheets or Forms.

What we look for

  • Comfortable with Google Meet and Google Classroom
  • Organised and reliable
  • Spreadsheet or basic scripting skills are a plus
Teaching Team

Teaching, Mentoring & Content Roles

For students with strong subject knowledge who want to help others learn.

Teaching Assistant – Foundation (Classes 1–8)

Support live classes, check homework and worksheets, and help younger students with practice and doubts under the founder's guidance.

What we look for

  • Strong school-level fundamentals in Science, English or Social Science
  • Patience and clear communication
  • Currently pursuing or completed graduation

Biology Subject Mentor (Classes 9–12)

Assist with doubt-solving sessions, prepare practice questions and PYQ sets, and support revision for board and NEET Biology aspirants.

What we look for

  • B.Sc. / M.Sc. student in Zoology, Botany, Life Sciences or related field
  • Sound NCERT Biology knowledge
  • Ability to explain diagrams and concepts clearly

Academic Content Creator

Draft chapter-wise notes, worksheets, Daily Practice Problems and assessments following the academy's format and quality standards.

What we look for

  • Strong subject knowledge in the chosen area
  • Clear, accurate written English
  • Comfortable with Google Docs and basic formatting

English Grammar & Vocabulary Tutor

Conduct practice sessions, check written work and build vocabulary exercises for learners in the Language Development programme.

What we look for

  • Excellent English grammar and vocabulary
  • Experience teaching, tutoring or peer mentoring
  • Encouraging, learner-friendly approach
