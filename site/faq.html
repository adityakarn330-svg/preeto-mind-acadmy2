Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our online classes, study materials, assessments and admission process.

Are classes conducted online?

Yes. All PreetoMind Academy classes are conducted live online. Students attend interactive classes from home through Google Meet.

Which classes and subjects are available?

We offer six academic departments: Foundation Education for Classes 1–8 (all subjects); Secondary Education for Classes 9–10 (all subjects except Mathematics); Senior Secondary Biology for Classes 11–12; Competitive Exam Preparation for NEET Biology, CUET UG and CUET PG; Higher Education support for B.Sc. subjects; and Language Development in English Grammar and English Vocabulary. See the Courses page for details.

How are live classes conducted?

Classes are held live on Google Meet. They are interactive: students are encouraged to take part, ask questions and clear doubts during class. Recorded lectures are not part of our programme. Learning happens through live teaching supported by study materials and practice.

Will students receive notes and worksheets?

Yes. Enrolled students receive chapter-wise notes, worksheets, practice assignments, Daily Practice Problems (DPPs) and Previous Year Questions (PYQs) where relevant. These are shared privately through Google Classroom and designated learning channels.

Are weekly tests conducted?

Yes. Weekly tests and assessments are a regular part of our academic system. They help us check understanding, identify learning gaps and plan revision.

How can parents track student progress?

Parents receive monthly performance reports and regular progress updates. We also keep in touch with parents directly, so they always know how their child is doing and where support is needed.

How can students enrol?

Submit an admission enquiry or contact us directly. We will discuss your academic requirements, arrange a demo class and share course fee details directly. Admission is completed only after confirmation by the founder. Submitting an enquiry does not confirm admission.

How can parents contact the founder?

Parents can contact PreetoMind Academy directly by WhatsApp or email. Details are on the Contact Us page.

How are class routines and planners shared?

After admission, students receive their academic planner and class routine by email. Google Classroom access is shared through the student’s registered email address, and weekly academic updates are shared through the designated learning channels.

How can students attend a demo class?

Contact us through the Admission Enquiry form, WhatsApp or email. After we understand the student’s requirements, we will arrange a suitable demo class.

Still have a question? Contact PreetoMind Academy directly.

Interested in joining PreetoMind Academy?

Contact us directly for admissions, course details, and further guidance.
