Why Choose PreetoMind Academy?

Structured Teaching. Personal Guidance. Steady Progress.

Students do not just attend classes at PreetoMind Academy. They learn within a complete academic system that covers clear teaching, regular practice, assessment and feedback.

Our Teaching Methodology

Concept-based teaching

Every topic is taught from the basics, so students understand the reasons as well as the facts.

Live interactive online classes

Real-time classes on Google Meet where students take part and ask questions freely.

Personalised student attention

Guidance and practice adapted to each learner’s needs and pace.

Chapter-wise study materials

Well-organised notes for every chapter to support learning and revision.

Worksheets and assignments

Structured practice that reinforces each concept after it is taught.

Daily Practice Problems

Short, regular DPPs that build consistency and accuracy.

Weekly assessments

Regular tests that show students where they stand.

Regular doubt-solving

Dedicated time to clear doubts before they become learning gaps.

Academic progress monitoring

Continuous tracking of each student’s performance and improvement.

Exam-oriented preparation

Focused preparation for school, board, university and competitive examinations.

Meet the Founder

Preety Kumari

Founder – PreetoMind Academy | Educator | Creative Writer
Biology & ScienceEnglish Grammar & VocabularyLiterary Award Recipient

Preety Kumari founded PreetoMind Academy to give students what she believes every learner deserves: clear concepts, consistent practice and personal academic attention. With a B.Sc. in Zoology with Research, she brings a strong scientific foundation to her teaching of Biology and Science.

Alongside her experience in online tutoring, she teaches English Grammar and Vocabulary and creates educational content for her students. She is also a creative writer and a recipient of literary recognition. This mix of scientific discipline and creative expression shapes how she teaches: precise and thorough, but also engaging and encouraging.
