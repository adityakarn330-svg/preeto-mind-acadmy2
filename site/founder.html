About the Founder – Preety Kumari

Educator, creative writer and the academic leader behind PreetoMind Academy.

Preety Kumari

Founder and Academic Educator, PreetoMind Academy
Creative WriterLiterary Award Recipient

Preety Kumari is the founder and academic educator of PreetoMind Academy. She leads the academy’s teaching, curriculum planning and student guidance, and she is closely involved in every learner’s progress.

With a background in Zoology with Research, experience in online tutoring and a deep interest in language and literature, she combines scientific precision with creative communication. Her commitment to education is personal. She believes that dedicated teaching, structured practice and genuine care can change how a student learns.
