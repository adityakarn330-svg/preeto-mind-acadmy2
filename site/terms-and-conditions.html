1. General Website Usage

By using this website, you agree to these Terms and Conditions. The website gives information about PreetoMind Academy’s courses, teaching methods and admission process. Please use it lawfully and do not misuse its content or features.

2. Course and Academic Information

We aim to keep course and academic information on this website accurate and up to date. Course content, subjects, schedules and academic arrangements may be updated from time to time. For the latest details, please contact the academy directly.

3. Admission Enquiry and Confirmation

Submitting an admission enquiry does not confirm admission. The admission process includes discussing academic requirements, attending a demo class, and confirming course fees directly with the founder. Admission is complete only after confirmation by the founder, who may approve or decline admission at her discretion.

4. Course Fees

Course fees are not published on this website. All fee details are shared and agreed directly with the founder before admission. PreetoMind Academy does not collect payments through this website.

5. Class Schedules and Communication

Live classes are held on Google Meet. Enrolled students receive their academic planner and class routine by email. Google Classroom access is shared through the student’s registered email address. Students and parents should keep their registered contact details up to date and check them regularly for academic updates.

6. Study Material Usage and Copyright

All study materials, notes, worksheets, DPPs, assessments and other content provided by PreetoMind Academy are the intellectual property of the academy and are for the personal, non-commercial use of enrolled students only. They must not be copied, shared, sold, uploaded or distributed without written permission. Website content, including text, logo and design, is also protected and may not be reused without permission.

7. Conduct During Online Classes

  • Join classes on time using the registered account or link provided
  • Behave respectfully towards the teacher and other students
  • Do not record, photograph, screenshot or share classes or class materials without permission
  • Do not share class links or Google Classroom access with anyone else
  • Use chat and microphone features appropriately and only for academic purposes

PreetoMind Academy may take appropriate action, including removal from a class, if these guidelines are not followed.

8. Changes to Academic Arrangements

Where necessary, PreetoMind Academy may reschedule classes, adjust timings, or modify course plans or academic arrangements. We will inform students and parents of any such changes through the registered communication channels as early as reasonably possible.

9. Limitation

We are committed to quality teaching and academic support. However, examination results depend on many factors, including the student’s own effort, so specific marks or outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

10. Changes to These Terms

We may update these Terms and Conditions from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
