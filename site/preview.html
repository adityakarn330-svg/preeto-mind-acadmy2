About PreetoMind Academy

A founder-led online educational institution built on quality teaching, strong conceptual foundations and personalised academic guidance.

Our Institution

Quality Education, Delivered Personally

PreetoMind Academy is a founder-led online educational institution that supports learners at every stage, including school education, Senior Secondary Biology, competitive examinations, higher education and language development.

The academy was built on a simple belief: students achieve more when they truly understand what they learn. Every class is planned to build strong conceptual foundations, followed by consistent academic practice, regular assessment and clear feedback.

Because the academy is founder-led, teaching stays student-centred. Each learner is known individually, their progress is followed closely, and their guidance is adapted to their needs. Parents stay informed through regular communication and progress updates.

The goal is not only better marks, but lasting academic confidence: students who can think clearly, express themselves well and prepare for examinations with assurance.

Academic Monitoring

Student Assessment and Progress System

A continuous, structured process that tracks progress, identifies learning gaps early and supports steady improvement.

Our Approach

Continuous Improvement, Not Marks Alone

Marks are one measure of learning, but not the only one. Our assessment system looks at understanding, consistency, effort and improvement over time.

Each assessment is used to guide what happens next, whether that is more practice, revision, a change of approach or extra support. Students and parents always know where things stand and what the next step is.

Every test is a step towards improvement, not just a score.

Regular homework and practice

Students complete homework, worksheets and DPPs to reinforce what they learn in each class.

Weekly tests and assessments

Weekly tests check understanding, retention and how well students apply concepts.

Performance analysis

Test results and classwork are reviewed to understand each student’s strengths and challenges.

Monthly progress reports

A clear monthly summary of performance, effort and areas for improvement.

Identification of learning gaps

Specific topics or skills that need attention are identified early.

Revision and additional academic support

Targeted revision, extra practice and doubt-solving to close learning gaps.

Parent communication and progress updates

Parents are kept informed so they can support their child’s learning at home.

Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our online classes, study materials, assessments and admission process.

Are classes conducted online?

Yes. All PreetoMind Academy classes are conducted live online. Students attend interactive classes from home through Google Meet.

Which classes and subjects are available?

We offer six academic departments: Foundation Education for Classes 1–8 (all subjects); Secondary Education for Classes 9–10 (all subjects except Mathematics); Senior Secondary Biology for Classes 11–12; Competitive Exam Preparation for NEET Biology, CUET UG and CUET PG; Higher Education support for B.Sc. subjects; and Language Development in English Grammar and English Vocabulary. See the Courses page for details.

How are live classes conducted?

Classes are held live on Google Meet. They are interactive: students are encouraged to take part, ask questions and clear doubts during class. Recorded lectures are not part of our programme. Learning happens through live teaching supported by study materials and practice.

Will students receive notes and worksheets?

Yes. Enrolled students receive chapter-wise notes, worksheets, practice assignments, Daily Practice Problems (DPPs) and Previous Year Questions (PYQs) where relevant. These are shared privately through Google Classroom and designated learning channels.

Are weekly tests conducted?

Yes. Weekly tests and assessments are a regular part of our academic system. They help us check understanding, identify learning gaps and plan revision.

How can parents track student progress?

Parents receive monthly performance reports and regular progress updates. We also keep in touch with parents directly, so they always know how their child is doing and where support is needed.

How can students enrol?

Submit an admission enquiry or contact us directly. We will discuss your academic requirements, arrange a demo class and share course fee details directly. Admission is completed only after confirmation by the founder. Submitting an enquiry does not confirm admission.

How can parents contact the founder?

Parents can contact PreetoMind Academy directly by WhatsApp or email. Details are on the Contact Us page.

How are class routines and planners shared?

After admission, students receive their academic planner and class routine by email. Google Classroom access is shared through the student’s registered email address, and weekly academic updates are shared through the designated learning channels.

How can students attend a demo class?

Contact us through the Admission Enquiry form, WhatsApp or email. After we understand the student’s requirements, we will arrange a suitable demo class.

How We Teach

Teaching Methodology

Three core approaches that help every PreetoMind Academy student understand deeply, practise consistently and progress with confidence.

Approach 01

Concept-Based Learning

Concepts are explained from the basics using simple language, examples, illustrations and structured explanations.

  • Start from fundamentals before moving to advanced ideas
  • Simple language and relatable, real-life examples
  • Diagrams, illustrations and visual explanations
  • Structured, step-by-step progression through each chapter
  • Regular connections between topics for deeper understanding
Approach 02

Interactive Live Classes

Interactive online classes are conducted through Google Meet, encouraging participation, questions and regular doubt-solving.

  • Live, real-time teaching on Google Meet
  • Students are encouraged to participate and ask questions
  • Regular doubt-solving built into every class
  • Quick checks for understanding during lessons
  • A focused, disciplined and respectful online classroom
Approach 03

Personalised Student Attention

Academic guidance and practice are adapted to each student’s learning needs and progress.

  • Understanding each learner’s strengths and challenges
  • Practice adapted to individual pace and level
  • Targeted support for identified learning gaps
  • Ongoing monitoring of progress
  • Regular communication with parents
The Learning Cycle

From Understanding to Improvement

Every topic moves through a structured cycle so that learning is reinforced, checked and improved.

Learn

Concept explained clearly in a live class.

Practise

Worksheets, assignments and DPPs.

Assess

Weekly tests and performance analysis.

Review

Doubt-solving and revision of weak areas.

Improve

Progress shared with students and parents.
