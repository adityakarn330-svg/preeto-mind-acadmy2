How this dashboard works

Enquiries and reviews arrive in your Gmail. Use this page to keep track of them and to choose which reviews appear on the website.

  1. Copy a student's review from Gmail into Reviews and tick Published.
  2. Click Download reviews.json.
  3. Upload that file to site/reviews.json in GitHub. Vercel updates the site automatically.

Data is saved only in this browser. Use Backup to keep a copy or move to another device.

Add a review
