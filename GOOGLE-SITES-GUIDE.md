# PreetoMind Academy: Google Sites Build Guide

The working prototype (folder `site/`) is the design and content reference. Open each prototype page next to Google Sites and copy the text section by section. This guide covers setup, layout and the features that Google Sites handles differently.

**Brand:** PREETOMIND ACADEMY · LEARN • GROW • EXCEL · *Building Strong Foundations, Creating Bright Futures.*

---

## 1. Create the site

1. Go to **sites.google.com** using the academy's Google account (ideally the same account that will own the enquiry form and email).
2. Click **+ Blank site**. Set the site name to **PreetoMind Academy**.
3. **Publish → Web address:** choose something like `preetomind-academy` (gives `https://sites.google.com/view/preetomind-academy`). You can connect a custom domain later in **Settings → Custom domains**.

## 2. Brand settings

| Setting | Where | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Logo | Settings → Brand images → Logo | The official PreetoMind Academy logo (PNG with transparent background). `site/assets/preetomind-logo.svg` is only a placeholder. Use the real logo. |
| Favicon | Settings → Brand images → Favicon | Square version of the logo |
| Theme | Themes panel | A clean theme such as **Simple** or **Vision** |
| Theme colour | Themes → colour → custom | Orange **#E8731A** (dark-orange accents **#C65F10**) |
| Dark sections | Section → Background → **Emphasis 2/3** | Gives the charcoal look (theme-dependent) |
| Fonts | Themes → Font style | Choose the closest clean sans-serif style the theme offers. The prototype uses Montserrat (headings) and Open Sans (body). Google Sites only offers each theme's built-in font styles. |

Brand palette: Charcoal **#1E1F22** · Orange **#E8731A** · White **#FFFFFF** · Light neutral **#F6F4F1**

## 5. Page-by-page layout

Use **Insert → Layouts** (two or three-column cards) for card grids. Use **Insert → Button** for buttons, **Insert → Collapsible group** for FAQs, and **Insert → Divider** between sections. Copy the wording from the matching prototype page.

**Home**
1. **Header banner** (Header type: *Large banner*, dark background image or Emphasis colour): "PREETOMIND ACADEMY", headline, tagline, intro paragraph. Add buttons **Explore Our Courses** (→ Courses) and **Contact Us Directly for Admissions** (→ Contact).
2. Academic Departments: six cards (3-column layout × 2) with icon image, level, name and one line, each linking to Courses.
3. Why Choose PreetoMind Academy?: the ten points as a 2-column list or small cards.
4. Founder introduction: photo on the left, name, "Founder – PreetoMind Academy | Educator | Creative Writer", introduction, button → Founder page.
5. Learning Support: ten items (dark section).
6. Testimonials: **add only when genuine testimonials exist**. Until then, hide this section or show one line: "Genuine feedback from our students and parents will appear here."
7. Contact & Admission: "Interested in joining PreetoMind Academy?" with WhatsApp and Email buttons.

**About PreetoMind Academy:** institutional introduction, What We Emphasise list, Vision and Mission (two cards), eight Educational Values (4-column × 2), departments.

**About the Founder:** photo, name, designation and identity, introduction, eight subsections (Qualifications, Teaching Experience, Subject Expertise, English teaching, Certifications, Content Creation, Literary Journey, Teaching Philosophy).

**Courses:** fee notice at the top, then one section per department with level, subjects, introduction, focus list, and buttons (Enquire → Admission Enquiry; Ask on WhatsApp).

**Teaching Methodology:** three approaches (Concept-Based Learning, Interactive Live Classes, Personalised Student Attention), the learning cycle (Learn → Practise → Assess → Review → Improve) and the Online Learning & Communication section (Google Meet, private Google Classroom, planner by email). **Do not** add a public Classroom link or student login.

**Study Materials:** a notice that full materials are only for enrolled students, then six sample cards. To add a real sample, upload a PDF to Google Drive, set sharing to **Anyone with the link → Viewer**, then **Insert → Drive** to embed a preview. Share only samples you are happy to make public.

**Assessment & Progress:** seven-step monitoring process, the "Continuous improvement, not marks alone" message, and twelve support facilities.

**Testimonials:** genuine feedback only, with first name (with permission), class or course, feedback, and an optional photo (with consent). No ratings or invented reviews.

**Admission Enquiry:** five-step admission process, the "enquiry does not confirm admission" notice, the fee notice, and the embedded Google Form (section 7).

**Join Our Team:** introduction and role-details card (fill in engagement type, duration, stipend and weekly hours), four Tech Team role cards, four Teaching Team role cards, the four-step "How to Apply" process, and an embedded Google Form (same approach as section 7) with: Full Name, Email, Mobile/WhatsApp, City, College/Course/Year, Team, Preferred Role, Skills, Availability, CV/Portfolio link, "Why do you want to join?", consent checkbox.

**FAQs:** **Insert → Collapsible group**, one per question (copy the ten Q&As from the prototype).

**Contact Us:** two cards (WhatsApp, Email) with buttons, plus the statement "For admissions, course information, and academic enquiries, please contact PreetoMind Academy directly."

**Privacy Policy / Terms and Conditions:** copy from the prototype, then **review and adjust them to the academy's actual practice** (fill in the yellow placeholders, such as the form service used and the retention period).

## 7. Admission Enquiry Form (Google Forms)

> **Static site (zip) note:** in the static website both forms (Admission Enquiry and Team Application) are already connected through **FormSubmit** (`https://formsubmit.co/preetomindacadmy@gmail.com`) and email every submission to **preetomindacadmy@gmail.com** with the reply-to set to the sender. FormSubmit emails a one-time **Activate** link to that Gmail after the first submission; click it once and the forms are live. Google Sites cannot host custom HTML forms, so the Google Sites version uses Google Forms as below.


1. In **Google Forms**, create **"PreetoMind Academy – Admission Enquiry"** with these questions:
   * Student's Full Name (Short answer, required)
   * Parent / Guardian Name (Short answer, required)
   * Student's Class / Course (Dropdown: Class 1–5, Class 6–8, Class 9–10, Class 11–12 Biology, NEET Biology, CUET UG, CUET PG, B.Sc. Subjects, English Grammar & Vocabulary, Other; required)
   * School / College Name (Short answer)
   * Subjects Interested In (Short answer or Checkboxes, required)
   * Parent's Mobile Number (Short answer, required; use **Response validation → Regular expression** if you want to check the format)
   * Email Address (Short answer, **Response validation → Text → Email**, required)
   * Preferred Class Timing (Multiple choice: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekend, Flexible)
   * Current City (Short answer, required)
   * Additional Message / Query (Paragraph)
   * Consent checkbox: "I agree that PreetoMind Academy may use these details to respond to this enquiry, as described in the Privacy Policy." (required)
2. **Settings → Presentation → Confirmation message:** "Thank you. Your admission enquiry has been received. PreetoMind Academy will contact you soon. Please note that submitting an enquiry does not confirm admission."
3. Rename the submit button. Google Forms **does not allow** changing the "Submit" button text. To use the label **Submit Admission Enquiry**, place it as the heading directly above the embedded form, or add a final section titled "Submit Admission Enquiry".
4. **Email to the founder:** in **Responses → ⋮ → Get email notifications for new responses**. This sends an alert to the form owner's email, but the email does **not** include the answers. To receive the full enquiry details by email, either:
   * install a reputable Forms add-on that emails the responses, or
   * add a short Google Apps Script with an *On form submit* trigger that emails the responses.
5. **Responses → Link to Sheets** keeps a record of all enquiries.
6. In Google Sites: **Insert → Forms**, select the form, and resize it to full width.

## 8. SEO in Google Sites: what is and isn't possible

| Requirement | In Google Sites |
|---|---|
| SEO-friendly page titles | Supported. The page name becomes the title. Use descriptive names (e.g. "Courses and Academic Departments"). |
| Meaningful URLs | Supported, using custom paths (section 3). |
| Clear headings | Supported. Use Title / Heading / Subheading styles in order. |
| Image alt text | Supported. Select the image → **⋮ → Alt text**. Suggested alt text is in the prototype's HTML. |
| Meta descriptions | **Not supported.** Google Sites does not let you set per-page meta descriptions. Search engines use page text, so make each page's first paragraph a clear summary. |
| Structured data (schema) | Not supported. |
| Analytics | **Settings → Analytics** with a Google Analytics measurement ID. |
| Search Console | Can be verified through the Google Analytics method or a custom domain (DNS). |

Use the keywords naturally in headings and text (the prototype copy already does this): PreetoMind Academy, online classes, online tuition, Biology classes, Science classes, NEET Biology preparation, CUET preparation, B.Sc. academic support, English Grammar classes, English Vocabulary classes, online education. Avoid keyword stuffing.

## 9. Other Google Sites limitations

* **Embed code** (Insert → Embed → Embed code) shows custom HTML inside a sandboxed frame. Use it sparingly. Content inside it is not treated as page text for SEO.
* **Custom CSS / fonts / hover effects** are not supported. Spacing, cards and colours follow the chosen theme.
* **Mobile:** Google Sites layouts are responsive automatically. Check each page with **Preview → phone/tablet**.
* **Alternative:** the prototype in `site/` is a complete static website. If the academy later wants full design control (custom fonts, a floating WhatsApp button, meta descriptions), it can be hosted for free on GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages instead of Google Sites.
